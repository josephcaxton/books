# Back Cover Synopsis

**Some wisdom can only be earned through pain. This book exists so yours doesn't have to be.**

Joseph Caxton-Idowu spent decades learning the hard way through crushing environments, failed ventures, betrayals by people he trusted, and years lost to living without clear direction. Every lesson cost him something: time, money, relationships, or opportunities he can never recover.

This book is the inheritance he refuses to let die with him.

Written first for his children and the generations that will carry his name, *The Price I Paid* distils a lifetime of experience into twelve direct, practical principles for building a purposeful life from discovering what you were made for, to protecting your vision, mastering your craft, guarding your time, and learning who to trust before the damage is done.

Each chapter delivers the same three things: the lesson life forced him to learn, the raw personal story behind it, and his direct advice to you.

No fluff. No theory. Just the wisdom of a man who paid full price offered to you at no cost.
