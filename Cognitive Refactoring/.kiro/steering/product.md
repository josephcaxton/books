# Product Vision: Cognitive Refactoring

## Core Objective
To provide knowledge workers and organizational leaders with a repeatable, engineering grade framework to systematically audit, unlearn, and refactor their human operating systems transitioning them from vulnerable task executors into irreplaceable strategic architects in an AI-driven economy."

## Target Audience Themes
- Primary: The Vulnerable Knowledge Worker, the Daily Operator, Tech Pragmatist, and the Career Defender
- Secondary: The Enterprise Leader & Talent Architect.

## Non-Goals (What NOT to write)
- DO NOT write coding tutorials, Python scripts, or API setup guides.
- DO NOT use basic, outdated 2023 fluff (e.g., "How to write a prompt for marketing").
- NOT a Sci-Fi Dystopian Thriller or Doomsday Warning
- NOT a "Tech-Bro" AI Hype Manual or Utopian Pitch
- NOT a Transient Software Manual or Prompt Cheat Sheet
- NOT an Academic Computer Science or AI Engineering Textbook
- NOT Passive, Feel-Good Self-Help
