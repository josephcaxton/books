# About the Author

**Joseph Caxton-Idowu** is a Forward Deployed AI and Cloud Engineer with over twenty years of experience in enterprise technology, delivering cloud transformation and AI-native architecture across financial services, insurance, media, and technology. That work has repeatedly placed him where AI meets regulation, risk and accountability: the same intersection this book is about.

Joseph's career spans some of the most recognised organisations in the United Kingdom and Europe including Aviva, Tata Consultancy Services, Barclaycard, Avanade, Barclays Bank, eBay Kleinanzeigen, Deloitte, BBC, Transport for London, Sky, Bauer Media Group, and Provident Financial. His work at Aviva, Barclaycard and Provident Financial placed him inside heavily regulated banking, insurance and consumer finance environments, where model risk, data protection, auditability and human oversight are not abstractions but daily operating constraints the same constraints the governance mechanisms in this book are designed to meet.

He is the founder of **First Cloud Solutions**, through which he brings enterprise grade cloud and AI engineering directly to mid-size and large organisations navigating complex transformation. He is also the founder of **LearnersCloud** ([learnerscloud.com](https://learnerscloud.com)), an adaptive AI-driven GCSE and iGCSE learning platform used by students worldwide a product he re-engineered from a passive video platform into an intelligent system with real-time student profiling and automated remediation, where responsible handling of data about young learners made governance a design requirement rather than an afterthought.

Joseph's technical expertise spans the full breadth of modern cloud and AI engineering: large scale AWS migrations, Kubernetes platform design, cloud security transformation, DevOps delivery, and forward deployed AI engineering embedding within enterprise product and operations teams to design agentic workflows, retrieval pipelines and the controls that surround them. At Barclaycard, he led the integration of AI-augmented pipelines into a mission critical payment gateway, an environment where every change carried real regulatory and operational risk.

He holds five AWS certifications: DevOps Engineer Professional, Solutions Architect Professional, Security Specialty, Advanced Networking Specialty, and AI Practitioner.

The ideas in this book are not theoretical. They emerge from years of forward deployment inside enterprises designing the operating models, risk classifications, lifecycle controls, assurance evidence and accountability structures that this book describes as governing AI responsibly at scale, independent of any single cloud, model or vendor.
