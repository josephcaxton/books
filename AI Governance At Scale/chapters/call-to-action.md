# Work With Me

If the ideas in this book resonated, if you recognise that enterprise AI governance is an operating model problem rather than a principles problem and want to move your organisation from an ethics statement to a governed, assured, production system, I would welcome the conversation.

Through **First Cloud Solutions**, I deliver forward deployed AI and cloud engineering for mid-size and large organisations. Not only advisory. Not slide decks. Embedded delivery: designing the operating models, lifecycle controls and accountability structures described in this book, and standing them up alongside your governance, risk and engineering teams so they actually work in production.

**What I deliver:**

- **AI Governance Design** — Building the mechanisms described in this book: risk classification and intake, governance operating models, lifecycle control points, human oversight, monitoring and incident response, and the assurance evidence auditors and regulators expect. Platform neutral, so the controls hold across any cloud, model or vendor.
- **Regulatory and Assurance Alignment** — Translating cross cutting instruments (EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001) and sector specific regimes (financial services, healthcare, public sector, employment, critical infrastructure) into practical, proportionate controls and demonstrable evidence.
- **Responsible AI in Practice** — Turning fairness, transparency, privacy, safety and contestability from principles into enforceable controls, with the monitoring and documentation to prove they work.
- **Cloud Security and Governance** — Hardening the infrastructure your AI runs on, from identity and guardrails to threat modelling and policy enforcement. For dedicated security and compliance engagements, I partner with [Epitechnic](https://www.epitechnic.com).
- **Governance Advisory** — Helping governance, risk and leadership teams make the decisions: risk appetite, centralised versus federated operating models, control proportionality, and governance maturity for AI at scale.

Whether you are classifying your first high risk use case, deciding between centralised and federated governance, or building the function that lets many teams deploy AI safely, lawfully and accountably, let's have a direct conversation.
