# About the Author

**Joseph Caxton-Idowu** is a Forward Deployed AI and Cloud Engineer with over twenty years of experience in enterprise technology, delivering cloud transformation and AI-native architecture across financial services, insurance, media, and technology.

Joseph's career spans some of the most recognised organisations in the United Kingdom and Europe including Aviva, Tata Consultancy Services, Barclaycard, Avanade, Barclays Bank, eBay Kleinanzeigen, Deloitte, BBC, Transport for London, Sky, Bauer Media Group, and Provident Financial. His work at Aviva and BarclayCard positions him at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and AI within large scale banking, insurance and enterprise operations the same intersection where the architectural patterns in this book are tested daily in production.

He is the founder of **First Cloud Solutions**, through which he brings enterprise grade cloud and AI engineering directly to mid-size and large organisations navigating complex transformation. He is also the founder of **LearnersCloud** ([learnerscloud.com](https://learnerscloud.com)), an adaptive AI-driven GCSE and iGCSE learning platform used by students worldwide a product he re-engineered from a passive video platform into an intelligent system with real-time student profiling and automated remediation.

Joseph's technical expertise spans the full breadth of modern cloud and AI engineering: large scale AWS migrations, Kubernetes platform design, cloud security transformation, DevOps delivery, and forward deployed AI engineering embedding within enterprise product and operations teams to design agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and prompt architectures that connect advanced AI capability directly to business processes. At Barclaycard, he led the integration of AI-augmented pipelines into a mission critical payment gateway.

He holds five AWS certifications. DevOps Engineer Professional, Solutions Architect Professional, Security Specialty, Advanced Networking Specialty, and AI Practitioner.

The ideas in this book are not theoretical. They emerge from years of forward deployment inside enterprises designing the reference architectures, security and governance boundaries, multi account AI platforms, and operational models that this book describes as production grade Generative AI systems on AWS.
