# Synopsis

## The AWS GenAI Architect

---

Most enterprise Generative AI efforts do not fail because the model was too weak. They fail because there was no architecture around it. A capable foundation model, a clever prompt, and a working demonstration are not a system. They are the beginning of a systems engineering problem that most organisations have not yet recognised as one.

**The AWS GenAI Architect** is the field guide for closing that gap.

It gives architects and engineers a complete way to reason about enterprise Generative AI on AWS, built on a single argument: GenAI is an architecture discipline. The model is one component within a larger system, and the quality of that system, not the quality of the model, determines whether AI delivers value safely, reliably, governably, and economically at scale. No hype. No model leaderboard chasing. Every pattern is examined as an architecture review, with its trade-offs made explicit and a clear verdict on when to reach for it and when to leave it alone.

The book moves through six parts: **Thinking as a GenAI Architect** (the bridge from cloud architecture, the enterprise problem space, and the role of foundation models and Amazon Bedrock), **The Core Patterns** (direct integration, the AI Gateway, RAG and advanced retrieval, model routing, and agentic systems), **The Enterprise Platform** (federated multi-account AI, reference architecture, platform engineering, and networking), **Security, Governance, and Responsible AI** (identity, guardrails, threat modelling, and federated governance), **Production-Grade Operations** (resilience, observability, evaluation, performance, and the economics of GenAI), and **Judgement** (reasoning about trade-offs, a worked case study, and designing for a landscape that keeps evolving).

Written for every architect who has watched the proof of concept impress the room, then faced the harder question on Monday morning: "It works for one user with my permissions over clean data. Now how do I make it safe, reliable, governable, and affordable for the whole enterprise?"

The model is a component. The architecture is the product.
