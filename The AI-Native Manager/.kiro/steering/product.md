# Product Vision: The AI-Native Manager

## Core Objective
To provide managers, team leads, and delivery directors with an operational playbook for redesigning how they lead teams when AI dissolves the fundamental constraint management was built to optimise: limited human execution capacity. The book delivers a complete management operating system for the AI-augmented era — covering work design, team architecture, people development, performance measurement, and organisational navigation.

## Series Position
This is Book 3 of the AI Transformation Trilogy:
- Book 1 (The AI-Native Executive) addresses the C-suite and enterprise architecture layer.
- Book 2 (Cognitive Refactoring) addresses the individual knowledge worker and their cognitive adaptation.
- Book 3 (The AI-Native Manager) addresses the team and operational layer where strategy meets execution and where most organisational value is created or destroyed.

## Target Audience Themes
- Primary: Middle managers, team leads, engineering managers, product managers, delivery directors — anyone responsible for getting work done through other people in an AI-augmented environment.
- Secondary: Rising individual contributors transitioning into management who will inherit AI-native teams as their default reality.
- Tertiary: HR, L&D, and COOs building management development programmes for the AI era.

## Non-Goals (What NOT to Write)
- NOT a technology manual or AI tool tutorial
- NOT a prompt engineering guide for managers
- NOT a "how to use ChatGPT for management tasks" listicle
- NOT academic management theory or MBA curriculum
- NOT a futurist manifesto about artificial general intelligence
- NOT a generic leadership book with AI sprinkled on top
- NOT a cost-cutting playbook disguised as transformation guidance
- NOT a book that frames AI as a threat to management (it restructures management, it does not eliminate it)
