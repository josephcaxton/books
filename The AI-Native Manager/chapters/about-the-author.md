# About the Author

**Joseph Caxton-Idowu** is a Forward Deployed AI and Cloud Engineer with over twenty years of experience in enterprise technology, delivering cloud transformation and AI-native architecture across financial services, insurance, media, and technology.

Joseph's career spans some of the most recognised organisations in the United Kingdom and Europe including Aviva, Tata Consultancy Services, Barclaycard, Avanade, Barclays Bank, eBay Kleinanzeigen, Deloitte, BBC, Transport for London, Sky, Bauer Media Group, and Provident Financial. His work embeds him directly inside enterprise teams navigating the transition from legacy delivery models to AI-augmented operations, the exact terrain this book maps for managers and team leaders.

He is the founder of **First Cloud Solutions**, through which he brings enterprise grade cloud and AI engineering directly to mid-size and large organisations navigating complex transformation. He is also the founder of **LearnersCloud** ([learnerscloud.com](https://learnerscloud.com)), an adaptive AI-driven GCSE and iGCSE learning platform used by students worldwide, a product he re-engineered from a passive video platform into an intelligent system with real-time student profiling and automated remediation.

Joseph's technical expertise spans the full breadth of modern cloud and AI engineering: large scale AWS migrations, Kubernetes platform design, cloud security transformation, DevOps delivery, and forward deployed AI engineering, embedding within enterprise product and operations teams to design agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and prompt architectures that connect advanced AI capability directly to business processes.

He holds five AWS certifications: DevOps Engineer Professional, Solutions Architect Professional, Security Specialty, Advanced Networking Specialty, and AI Practitioner.

This is the third book in his AI Transformation Trilogy. The AI-Native Executive addresses enterprise strategy and infrastructure. Cognitive Refactoring addresses individual professional adaptation. The AI-Native Manager completes the picture at the team and operational layer, where strategy meets execution and where most organisational value is created or destroyed. The frameworks in this book emerge from two decades of working alongside managers, delivery directors, and team leads inside enterprises during successive waves of technological change, observing firsthand what separates the teams that adapt from those that calcify.
