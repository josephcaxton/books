# Synopsis

## The AI-Native Manager: Leading Teams When Execution Costs Zero

---

Management was invented to solve a scarcity problem: human execution capacity was expensive, limited, and required coordination. AI has dissolved that constraint. Every management practice built on execution scarcity, allocation, prioritisation, performance measurement, delegation, hiring must now be re-derived from first principles.

**The AI-Native Manager** is the operational playbook for that re-derivation.

It provides managers, team leads, and delivery directors with a complete framework for redesigning how they lead teams when execution is abundant, direction is scarce, and the primary human contribution is judgment rather than throughput. No hype. No tool tutorials. No generic leadership advice with AI sprinkled on top. Every chapter delivers a framework, a protocol, and a deployable management artefact.

The book moves through four parts: **The New Operating System** (what management becomes when execution costs zero), **Designing Work** (how to decompose, allocate, and orchestrate human-AI collaboration), **Leading People** (the psychology of role migration, judgment development, and performance architecture), and **Operating at Scale** (decision architecture, hiring, and navigating organisational resistance).

Book 3 of the AI Transformation Trilogy. The executive layer is covered. The individual layer is covered. This is the team layer where strategy meets execution, and where most organisational value is created or destroyed.

Written for every manager sitting at their desk on Monday morning asking: "My team can now produce three times the output. But three times the output of what?"
